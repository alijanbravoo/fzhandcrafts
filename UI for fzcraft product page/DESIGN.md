## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the delicate artistry of high-fashion floristry paired with modern boutique luxury e-commerce. Tailored for discerning patrons seeking everlasting handcrafted wearable botanicals, the design language communicates poise, artisanal mastery, and tactile indulgence.

The visual direction merges **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** with **Subtle Tactile Warmth**:
- **Purity and Airiness:** Expansive alabaster and linen negative space allows detailed synthetic petal textures and metallic hardware to command attention.
- **Editorial Sophistication:** High-fashion typographic scale creates an immediate sense of an atelier catalogue.
- **Subtle Organic Sensuality:** Pill-shaped interaction targets, hairline metallic borders, and whisper-quiet shadow gradients echo the curve and softness of preserved rose petals.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from botanical stems, rare rose varietals, and artisan metals:

- **Primary (`#1E4B3E` - Deep Botanical Green):** Serves as the anchor for primary conversion drivers, purchase CTAs, and authoritative typographic anchors.
- **Secondary (`#8B263E` - Vintage Rose Wine):** Reserved for intentional distinction—curated limited edition badges, focal status rings, active variant selections, and subtle luxury alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#C8963E` - Gilded Ochre):** Applied selectively to top-tier announcement banners, VIP membership markers, star ratings, and micro-embellishments.
- **Muted Sage (`#2D5A47`):** Utilized for hover transitions, secondary buttons, botanical tag fills, and secondary icons.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5` - Linen Cream):** Sets an organic, warm off-white canvas that prevents clinical eye fatigue while providing high contrast against pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card surfaces.
- **Hairline Accent (`#E8E2D9`):** A warm, low-contrast neutral border tone keeping structural separation discreet.

## Typography

The typographical pairing strikes an intentional tension between neoclassical grace and contemporary precision:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for heroic merchandising, story chapters, collection titles, and product names. Headings utilize tight negative tracking to maintain density and drama.
- **Operational Interface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Drives descriptions, technical specs, filter matrices, checkout sequences, and numerical price tags.
- **Letter Spacing Discipline:** All uppercase metadata, badge labels, and utility CTAs apply open tracking (`+0.05em` to `+0.1em`) to create breathing room and an artisanal sensibility. Prices are explicitly rendered in the geometric sans-serif to retain instant clarity during transaction paths.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a disciplined **12-column responsive grid** engineered for editorial pacing:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max-width bounded at 1440px with generous `margin` (`3rem`) and `gutter` (`1.5rem`). PDP (Product Detail Page) follows a split 7:5 asymmetric structure: 7 columns dedicated to immersive imagery and macro details, 5 columns dedicated to sticky purchasing controls.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with dynamic gutters (`1.25rem`) and container margins (`2rem`). PDP shifts to sequential flow with swipeable media cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with tight edge margins (`1.25rem`) maximizing visual real estate for wearable item details.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous multi-tiered vertical spaces (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) separate product storytelling sections, simulating luxury magazine layouts rather than cluttered commodity storefronts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is attained through **warm ambient lighting and low-contrast surface contrast** rather than stark drop shadows:

- **Canvas to Card Layering:** Surfaces rely on tonal shift. The base viewport sits on `#FAF8F5` (Linen Cream); actionable product cards and floating panels sit elevated upon crisp `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
- **Hairline Framing:** Cards and floating modules incorporate a delicate 1px boundary rendered in `#E8E2D9` (warm stone), providing structure without visual bulk.
- **Shadow Profiles:**
  - *Resting Cards:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 75, 62, 0.04)` — an ultra-diffused hint of botanical tint.
  - *Elevated Flyouts & Floating Sticky Cart:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 75, 62, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(139, 38, 62, 0.03)` — dual warm-toned ambient diffusion.
- **Glassmorphism Overlay:** Sticky top navigation and mobile bottom checkout bars utilize `rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)` with a `16px` backdrop blur, preserving contextual color without obscuring luxury imagery underneath.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **curved, organic geometry** inspired by the soft silhouettes of rose petals:

- **Interactive Controls (Level 3 - Full Pill):** Primary action buttons, filter tags, newsletter input containers, and status chips use full pill radiuses (`rounded-full` / `9999px`), inviting tactile engagement.
- **Product Surfaces & Structural Panels:** Cards, accordions, and image frame viewports employ soft curved bounds (`1rem` to `1.25rem`) that soften the viewport and distinguish the brand from sharp, stark fashion catalogs.
- **Micro-Controls:** Quantity selectors, color swatches, and thumbnail previews follow perfectly circular or harmonious elliptical ratios.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action (Add to Bag / Pre-order):** Solid `#1E4B3E` background, pure white typography, full pill radius, minimum height of 52px. Hover triggers a subtle transform (`scale(1.01)`) and transition to `#2D5A47`.
- **Secondary Action (Inquire / Save to Wishlist):** Transparent background with a 1px border in `#1E4B3E`, botanical green text. Hover fills surface with `rgba(30, 75, 62, 0.04)`.
- **Accent Badge / Exclusive CTA:** Solid `#8B263E` (Wine) used for limited bespoke runs or holiday reservation callouts.

### Product Detail Page (PDP) Swatches & Selectors
- **Color / Flower Material Swatches:** 36px circular swatches wrapped in a 1px `#E8E2D9` border. Selected state triggers an outward 2px offset ring in `#8B263E` (Wine) with a 2px interior whitespace buffer.
- **Size & Metal Finish Chips:** Pill-shaped capsules with 12px horizontal padding. Active state features a solid `#1E4B3E` background with white text; inactive state features transparent fill with `#E8E2D9` border and neutral slate body text.

### Badges & Notification Banners
- **Announcement Strip:** Sits at viewport apex in `#C8963E` (Ochre) background or `#FAF8F5` with gold borders; text formatted in `label-sm` uppercase.
- **Product Condition Badges (Handmade, Limited, Archive):** Pill-shaped chips resting over top-left photo coordinates. Fill is `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` with 1px border in `#8B263E` and Wine typography.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Fields (Checkout & Custom Inquiries):** Clean `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px `#E8E2D9` border, pill or 12px curvature. On focus, smoothly transitions border to `#1E4B3E` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 75, 62, 0.12)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular radio rings and soft-cornered checkboxes in `#1E4B3E`. Selected state displays custom micro-floral check mark or interior solid dot.

### Cards & Merchandising Surfaces
- **Product Listing Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background with 16px radius, enclosed by a 1px `#E8E2D9` border. Images use an editorial 4:5 aspect ratio. Hover reveals secondary angle (macro petal detail) via 350ms cross-fade, accompanied by an upward slide of a quick-add pill button.
- **Artisan Accordions:** Borderless rows separated by 1px warm-grey dividers. Header toggle features an elegant plus-to-minus morphing icon in `#1E4B3E`.
